About

I studied Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Ashesi University as a Mastercard Foundation Scholar and graduated in 2024. Since then I have stayed on as a Teaching and Research Assistant. I support graduate and undergraduate courses in Signals and Systems, Digital Signal Processing, Communication Systems and Engineering Calculus, and I work on research with faculty in the Engineering department.

I like work that ends in something people can use. My final-year project was a machine learning scarecrow that protects rice farms and runs fully offline on a Raspberry Pi. Since then, I have co-authored papers on wireless channel prediction and FPGA hardware acceleration, and I am exploring how signal processing can protect personal data in AI systems.

Teaching is a big part of my week. I teach GRE, GMAT and SAT mathematics at EducationUSA in Accra, and I post free engineering lessons on my YouTube channel, The Knowledge Centre.
