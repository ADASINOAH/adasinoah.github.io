About

I hold a BSc in Electrical & Electronic Engineering from Ashesi University (2024), where I was a MasterCard Foundation Scholar. My work sits at the intersection of power systems, signal processing, and machine learning.

My final-year capstone developed a YOLOv8-based electronic scarecrow that combines computer vision with low-cost electromechanical deterrents for agricultural protection. I have also worked on microgrid control, three-phase automatic changeover systems, cooperative spectrum sensing, digital signal processing (PCM), and privacy-preserving techniques for machine learning models.

I currently serve as a Teaching & Research Assistant at Ashesi University for graduate and undergraduate courses in Signals & Systems, Digital Signal Processing, Communication Systems, and Mechatronics. In parallel, I lecture GRE/GMAT/SAT Mathematics at EducationUSA and work as an AI for Health Research Fellow with the African Leadership Academy, investigating water quality in galamsey-affected communities in Ghana.

Outside the lab I led the Galaxy Space Campus Network at Ashesi as president, facilitated workshops and STEM outreach, and founded NPath Foundation, a non-profit supporting underprivileged students. I am actively seeking PhD opportunities in power systems, control of distributed energy resources, signal processing for trustworthy machine learning, and next-generation wireless systems.

Openings & Availability

My broader research interests lie in developing mathematical models, numerical methods, optimization, control, and signal-processing techniques for complex engineering and cyber-physical systems. I am particularly interested in:
  • Renewable-energy systems, microgrids, distributed energy resources, and resilient power systems
  • Optimization and control of energy and cyber-physical systems
  • Numerical methods and mathematical modeling for engineering applications
  • Signal processing and trustworthy machine learning
  • Next-generation wireless and communication systems
Across these areas, I am interested in using computational and data-driven methods to improve the reliability, security, sustainability, and social impact of engineering systems. I am also passionate about STEM education, climate resilience, and research that addresses broader societal challenges.

Please contact me by email if your research interests align with my background and goals.
