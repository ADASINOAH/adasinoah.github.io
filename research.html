Research

Research

My research interests focus on computational and data-driven engineering for complex cyber-physical systems. I am particularly interested in mathematical modeling, numerical methods, optimization, control, signal processing, communications, and trustworthy machine learning, with applications in renewable-energy systems, microgrids, power-system protection, and next-generation communication technologies. Through these methods, I aim to improve the privacy, reliability, efficiency, resilience, and broader societal impact of intelligent engineering systems.
