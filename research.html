Research

Research

My research uses signal processing and machine learning to solve practical problems: keeping personal data safe in AI systems, making wireless links more reliable, and building low-cost systems that work in places with little infrastructure. I work with faculty in the Engineering department at Ashesi University.

Current Work

Signal-Level Privacy for Machine Learning

Can data be protected before it ever reaches a model? With Prof. Hyder Ali Segu Mohamed, I am testing input-first quantization as a way to limit what a trained model leaks about its training data, while keeping it accurate.

QuantizationPrivacyDSP

Neural Channel Prediction for OTFS

Context-aware neural prediction of FIR filter parameters for OTFS-based wireless channels that change over time. Presented at IEEE ICAST 2026.

WirelessOTFSDeep Learning

Hardware Acceleration for Imaging

An FPGA-based FFT accelerator for detecting blur and restoring images in UAV surveillance. Co-authored with S. Agudu and S. Armah; presented at INTCEC 2026.

FPGAFFTImage Processing

Edge AI for Agriculture

A YOLOv8 detector running fully offline on a Raspberry Pi 4 to keep birds off rice farms, with 98% detection accuracy. Presented at BCUR 2026 in Glasgow.
